# Reading a page

## The layout

On a wide screen the app is a **left rail** of destinations beside the content;
the app bar across the top carries the brand, search, and your name. On a phone
the rail becomes a **bottom bar** of the same destinations.

## The colours

The dashboard uses four colour systems. None of them means "good" or "bad"
except **status**.

| System | What the colour means | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Status** | how a piece of work is going (the traffic-light) | status badges and bars |
| **Priority** | which annual priority something is (identity only) | priority dots and chips |
| **Goal** | which Strategy 2035 goal something aligns to | goal icons and cards |
| **Team** | which team is accountable | team swatches |

Status is the only one with a value judgement in it:

- **On track** — going to plan.
- **At risk** — a problem is visible; it needs attention.
- **Off track** — not going to plan.
- **Not started** — no work or no report yet.
- **Complete** — done.
- **Paused** — deliberately stopped for now.

Every status is also printed as a word beside its colour, so it reads without
colour vision and in greyscale print.

## Progress

Progress is **self-reported** by each initiative's owner: a percentage and a
status they set. It is an estimate, not a measurement, and the page says so.
Where an owner has not reported yet, a page shows "no progress reported yet"
rather than an empty bar that reads as zero.
