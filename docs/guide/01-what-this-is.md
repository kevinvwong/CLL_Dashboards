# What this dashboard is

The CLL Initiative Dashboard is the College of Lifetime Learning's strategy
portfolio, in one place. It answers three questions:

- **What work are we doing?** The 29 *Team Initiatives* — the College's bodies of
  work for the year — each owned by a person and aligned to one or more Strategy
  2035 goals.
- **How is it organised?** Five *Strategy 2035 goals*, six *annual priorities*, and
  four *teams*, and the links between them.
- **How is it going?** Progress that owners report themselves: a status and a
  percent complete per initiative, and the milestones reached for each priority.

It is a read-mostly dashboard. Owners update their own initiatives; everyone
signed in can read the whole portfolio.

## The shape of the model

```
Strategy 2035 goals (5)
        │  work aligns to
        ▼
Team Initiatives (29) ──── owned by ──── Teams (4) and People
        │  feeds
        ▼
Annual priorities (6) ──── reported as ──── Milestones
        │
        ▼
Dean Initiatives (11)
```

Nothing here is a score. The dashboard counts and lists; it does not invent a
composite number for "how the College is doing". Where a figure is a count, it
says so, and where data is missing it says that too.

## What it is not

- It is **not** a KPI system. A KPI has a unit, a numerator, a denominator and a
  target; the initiatives are *work*, not measurements. The words are kept
  strictly apart — see the [Glossary](/guide/glossary).
- It does **not** show figures it cannot stand behind. Until owners confirm
  their data, figures are labelled illustrative — see
  [Mock data vs confirmed data](/guide/mock-vs-confirmed).
