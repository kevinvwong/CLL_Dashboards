# The wireframes vs the package vs the prototype

Read 2026-10-06 from the three local sources in `Downloads`:
`CLL KPI Dashboard Wireframes and Mockups.pdf` (13 pages), the same title with
` (1)` (20 pages), and `Oct16_Wireframe_Data_Lists.xlsx`.

## What these documents are

A **staged programme**, not a schema and not a prototype:

```
Stage 1   Oct 16 2026   ONE wireframe, the Dean picks which
Stage 2   Apr 2027      college + 4 team pages + 6 priorities, on real data
Stage 3   Apr-Oct 2027  three levels linked: goals -> priorities -> Blueprint
```

The October 16 deliverable is explicitly **milestones reached of milestones
planned**, "instead of KPI values we cannot yet calculate", and explicitly
**not** the 2026 priorities view.

## The two versions disagree with each other

| | 13-page | 20-page |
|---|---|---|
| Options offered | **three** (A, B, C) each "1 of 3" | **two** (A, B) each "1 of 2" |
| C (Infinity drill-in) | a standalone option | merged into B |

The 20-page version reads as the later, narrowed revision: it adds the roadmap,
drops Option C into Option B, and moves the six-priorities view out of October 16
entirely. Its file timestamp is later. **Not confirmed** — both files were
downloaded in the same session and nothing states precedence.

One residue of the edit: the 20-page version still labels page 8 "Wireframe 2 of
3" while pages 9 and 10 say "of 2". A three-to-two narrowing that missed one page.

## The goal wording: three sources, three answers

The wireframes carry the disagreement explicitly:

> "Goal wording follows the May 13, 2026 deck. The earlier mockup differs on
> goals 1, 3 and 5; pending the Dean's decision."

| # | Package (canonical) | Prototype seed | Wireframes (May 13 deck) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Academic — "Catalyze a learning society..." | Academic | **Field-defining degrees at every level** |
| 2 | Extension — "Empower communities..." | Extension | Global network of 200 hubs |
| 3 | Learner — "Engage 5 million learners..." | **Research** | **Empower 5 million learners** |
| 4 | Research — "Be the global thought leader..." | **Learner impact** | $20M annual research |
| 5 | Operational — "Revolutionize an operational model..." | Operational | **Educate 10% of Fortune 500 L&D leaders** |

Three separate problems, not one:

- The **prototype has 3 and 4 transposed** against the package (blocker #3). Real.
- The **wireframes differ on 1, 3 and 5**, and say so. Not the same goals as the
  prototype's error.
- Goal 1's prototype title is a truncated paraphrase (already recorded).

So "fix the transposition" is not sufficient: if the May 13 deck wins, goals 1
and 5 also need rewriting, and "Academic / Extension / Learner / Research /
Operational" stop being the right short labels.

**This is a Dean decision.** The document says so in as many words.

## The priorities do not match at all

| Package / prototype `Priorities` table | Wireframes' six 2026 priorities |
|---|---|
| Culture | Culture |
| Scale | Financial Model |
| Identity | Operating Model |
| Innovation | Field of Study |
| Pathways | Content and Product |
| Data | Technology and AI |

Only **Culture** appears in both. The 20-page version names this unresolved:

> "Two sets of six priorities stay unreconciled"

This is the sharper problem. Our schema, our seed data, and the home screen's
six priority tiles are all built on one set; the programme the Dean is being
shown is built on the other.

## A third taxonomy layer we do not model

The wireframes add a layer between strategy and work that neither the prototype
nor Revision 2 has:

```
Strategy 2035 goals (5)
   |
2026 priorities (6)                    <- ours
   |
Dean outcomes P01-P06 (6)              <- Blueprint for 2027, NOT in our model
   |
4 teams, 29 KPIs (EXP/ECO/INF/FUT)    <- NOT in our model
   |
components (e.g. EXP-01a, EXP-01b)    <- NOT in our model
   |
milestones                            <- the Oct 16 unit of progress
```

Rev2 has Portfolio -> Goal -> Strategic Objective -> Target -> Initiative ->
Metric Version. The wireframes have Goal -> Priority -> Dean Outcome -> Team KPI
-> Component -> Milestone. **These are different shapes and neither contains the
other.**
