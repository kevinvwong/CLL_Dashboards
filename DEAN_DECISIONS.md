## Decision 1 — Which view for October 16?

**Question:** Which of the two views should be built and delivered on October 16?

**Background.** Both are static, clickable pages showing progress as milestones
reached of milestones planned. They differ in breadth, not in kind.

| | **A — six Dean outcomes** | **B — Georgia Tech Infinity in depth** |
|---|---|---|
| Shows | P01–P06, one card each | one initiative, goals → milestones → data |
| Data elements listed | 19 | 17 |
| Required for Oct 16 | 16 | 10 |
| Available now | 8 | 5 |
| Only partly available | 2 | 1 |
| Not collected yet | 6 | 4 |
| Deferred past Oct 16 | 0 | 5 |
| Items from owners | 20–30 milestones | 5 milestones |
| **People to ask** | **4 team leads** (one 30-minute session) | **1 owner** (Team 02 lead) |
| Realistic by Oct 16? | *"Yes, if scoped down"* | *"Yes"* |

**What the documents recommend.** The wireframes state their own read:

> *"B is the one we can deliver most honestly, and it becomes the template for
> the other priorities."*
> *"A works if team leads confirm milestones in one session instead of each owner
> reporting."*

**What each costs you.** A covers the whole portfolio but depends on **four
people** all responding in the same week. B depends on **one person** but shows a
single priority.

**A has a specific honesty cost, stated in its own trade-offs:**

> *"Does not match the 2026 priorities presented in May."*

**Also ask:** which version of the wireframes is current. Two are in circulation
and they disagree on how many options exist — the 13-page version offers **three**
(A outcomes, B priorities, C Infinity); the 20-page version offers **two** (A
outcomes, B Infinity) and moves the priorities view to Stage 2. Nothing in either
document states which supersedes the other.

*Source: the two wireframe PDFs; `Oct16_Wireframe_Data_Lists.xlsx`, "Choosing an
option" and the Option A/B sheets.*

## Decision 2 — Who owns each outcome or initiative?

**Question (if A):** Who is the named owner for each of the six Dean outcomes
P01–P06?
**Question (if B):** Who owns the Infinity initiative?

**Background.** This is **the blocker in both options**. The workbook's
`Blocking gaps` row reads:

- Option A: **"Outcome owners not named"**
- Option B: **"Initiative owner to confirm"**

The page cannot show a real status without an owner, because the status is
*self-reported by that person*. Until they are named, the page shows
`Owner: [name]`.

**The source says to settle this here.** Option A's data row A-04 records how the
owner is obtained:

> `Outcome owner` — provided by **"Elizabeth / Dean"** — how: **"Ask at the
> Dean's pick meeting"**

For Option B, row B-06 says the initiative owner is confirmed with Elizabeth.

**Cost of not deciding.** The plan has owners confirmed by **Oct 9**. Without a
name per outcome, the Oct 16 page carries placeholders where the whole value of
the view should be — *who is accountable*.

*Source: `Oct16_Wireframe_Data_Lists.xlsx`, rows A-04 and B-06, and the
`Blocking gaps` row.*

## Decision 3 — The annual priorities: now largely settled

**This was written as an open question. Reading your Blueprint prototype
(`cll-blueprint-2027.wag32002.chatgpt.site`) narrowed it, and it is worth stating
plainly what is and is not still open.**

**What is settled.** Three independent artifacts carry the *same six* priorities
word for word:

- your **Blueprint prototype** — P01–P06
- the **PMO KPI workbook** (`07_CLL_KPI_Atomic_Definitions_Master.xlsx`)
- the **October 16 wireframes**, Option A

| | |
|---|---|
| P01 | One Shared Identity |
| P02 | Champion Innovation |
| P03 | Integrated Portfolio & Pathways |
| P04 | Quality at Scale |
| P05 | Data-Informed Action |
| P06 | Culture & Learning |

The enterprise package's six — `Identity · Innovation · Pathways · Scale · Data ·
Culture` — are **the same six under short names**. Verified one by one: 6 of 6 map.
So there is not a competing second set here; there is one set named two ways.

**What remains open.** A *different* six appears in the wireframes' **Option B**,
citing the May 13 deck: `Culture · Financial Model · Operating Model · Field of
Study · Content and Product · Technology and AI`. Five of those six appear in
neither the prototype nor the package.

**Question:** is the May 13 deck's six still live, or superseded by the six your
prototype uses? If it is superseded, nothing needs doing. If it is still current,
the prototype and the PMO workbook are built on the wrong set.

**Why this matters despite not blocking October 16.** The October 16 page uses
P01–P06, which are the agreed six — so the deliverable is safe either way. But
Stage 2 will be built on one of these, and the two cannot both be right.

**Also worth settling, before the Board sees it:** the same six records are called
**"priorities"** in your prototype and the package, and **"Dean outcomes"** in the
PMO workbook and the wireframes. A reader cannot tell whether those are the same
thing. I believe they are, but the documents use both terms for one set.

*Source: `bp_data.js` from the prototype; `07_CLL_KPI_Atomic_Definitions_Master.xlsx`,
Metrics sheet; the package's `Priorities` sheet; the wireframes' Option A and B pages.*

## Decision 5 — Board endorsement of the wider programme

**Question:** Do you endorse the 3-to-6 month PMO implementation timeline and the
Data Steward Network?

**Background.** Separate from the October 16 view, the PMO package asks the Board
for something specific. From `04_dean_gaudelli_board_talking_points_v1.pdf`:

> *"Board Endorsement Requested: Approval of the 3-to-6 month PMO implementation
> timeline and endorsement of the Data Steward Network across college units to
> guarantee organizational accountability."*

Its Phase 0 gate is *"Endorsement of 3-6 mo roadmap."* Phase 1 then depends on
*"TCC approving targets and naming owners (mid-October)"* and a dedicated
SharePoint site and Power BI workspace.

**Note for the meeting:** the PMO specification claims its 35-KPI table
*"resolves the 21 previously unfinalized metrics."* The workbook does not support
that — its own `Class` column still marks **exactly 21 rows `Needs review`**,
only 8 are `Approved – source`, and all 40 are `Not started`. The definitions are
**written, not approved**. Worth knowing before endorsing anything that depends on
them.

*Source: `01_cll_pmo_gap_closure_master_v6.pdf`;
`04_dean_gaudelli_board_talking_points_v1.pdf`;
`07_CLL_KPI_Atomic_Definitions_Master.xlsx`, Metrics sheet.*
